Common questions

What does the client see in an estimate?

The detailed PDF shows selling prices by item. The summary PDF shows the scope and a single total. Internal costs and profit stay with your team.

How do I compare subcontractor bids?

Compare offers for the same work category and scope against the estimated cost and the price quoted to your client.
